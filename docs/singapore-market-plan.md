# Singapore Market Plan — Strategic Account Focus

Internal planning doc for sellers covering APAC / Singapore-based engineering hubs.

> **Note:** Market data below mixes **public patterns** with **hypotheses**. Validate all account-specific claims before customer conversations.

## Why Singapore matters

Singapore functions as a regional HQ and engineering hub for many global enterprises:

- Financial services (banks, insurance, fintech)
- Global tech regional offices
- Government-linked digital initiatives (Smart Nation ecosystem)

Buyers often run **global security policies** with **local data residency** concerns — relevant to AI coding tool evaluation.
