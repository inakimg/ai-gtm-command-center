# Discovery Questions — Seller Playbook

Reusable discovery question bank. Customize per account using Signal Radar output.

## Rules

- Questions **validate hypotheses** — they are not facts
- Lead with curiosity, not pitch
- Tag each question to a persona

---

## Opening (any persona)

1. What prompted you to take this meeting now?
2. How is your team using AI in the SDLC today — officially and unofficially?
3. If we nailed this project, what would be different in 6 months?

---

## VP Engineering / Engineering Director

1. Where do developers lose the most time today — reading code, writing code, or review?
2. How are you measuring productivity gains from AI tools?
3. What is blocking wider AI adoption — policy, quality, or skill gap?
4. Who decides which coding assistants are approved?

---

## VP AppSec / CISO organization

1. What is your policy on AI coding assistants today — allow, block, or case-by-case?
2. Have you seen sensitive code leave the governed environment (browser chat, personal tools)?
3. What would an ideal "approved path" for AI-assisted coding look like?
4. How do you review AI-generated pull requests differently?

---

## Platform / Developer Experience

1. How many AI coding tools are you supporting today?
2. What does your internal LLM gateway integrate with — IDE, CLI, both?
3. Where do developers complain most in Slack / tickets?
4. Are you evaluating build vs. buy for AI IDE standardization?

---

## Staff Engineer / Tech Lead (champion)

1. Walk me through your last refactor — where did AI help or hurt?
2. What would make you recommend a tool to your VP?
3. What breaks when juniors use AI without senior review?
4. Copilot vs. other tools — what do you reach for and why?

---

## Procurement / Commercial

1. Who owns the GitHub / Microsoft agreement that includes Copilot?
2. Is AI tooling centralized under one vendor or fragmented?
3. What is your typical pilot → production timeline?

---

## Shadow AI specific (Phase 4 prep)

1. Do engineers use ChatGPT or Claude in browser for code despite policy?
2. Is there a sanctioned internal alternative — and do people use it?
3. What audit evidence would you need for IDE-embedded AI?

---

## Competitive (neutral tone)

1. What tools are you comparing — and what criteria matter most?
2. What does "good enough" look like for your team?
3. What would make you stay with status quo?

---

## Closing

1. Who else should hear this conversation?
2. What is a low-risk next step — technical workshop, pilot scope, security review?
3. Can we agree on success criteria before a demo?

---

## How to use with Command Center

1. Run **Account Signal Radar** on target account
2. Copy hypothesis validation questions from radar output
3. Merge with persona sections above
4. Phase 6 will auto-generate customized sets from account context
