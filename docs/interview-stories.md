## Story 1: Shadow AI wake-up call

**Persona:** VP AppSec, financial services  
**Use when:** Job posts mention AI policy; AppSec leader is new

**Situation:** Global bank had approved Copilot but discovered heavy browser-based chat use for code snippets.

**Task:** CISO office wanted visibility without blocking developer velocity.

**Action:** Mapped governed IDE workflow; paired security review with DX pilot on non-production repo.

**Result:** *[Replace with your sourced outcome — e.g., "approved pilot scope" or "security review completed in X weeks"]*
